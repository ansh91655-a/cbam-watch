The European Commission has appointed today María Teresa Fábregas Fernández as Deputy Director-General General in the Reform and Investment Task Force (SG REFORM). This taskforce manages the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF), a cornerstone of NextGenerationEU, aimed at assisting EU Member States' economies in recovering from the Covid-19 crisis. This decision takes effect on 16 July 2026.
With over two decades of experience in negotiating landmark reforms, financial governance, and large-scale investment frameworks, Ms Fábregas Fernández has played a pivotal role in shaping and implementing Recovery and Resilience Plans, coordinating fiscal policies under the European Semester, and fostering close cooperation between Member States and EU institutions. Her career also includes pioneering work in tax policy, notably on the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism, where she has combined technical expertise with policy insight to advance the EU's green, digital, and social objectives. She has led multidisciplinary teams in the design, negotiation, and implementation of complex reform and investment programmes, working closely with Member States to ensure their effective delivery and to translate policy objectives into concrete results. María Teresa Fábregas Fernández, a Spanish national, is currently Director responsible for the establishment of the Reform and Investment Task Force (SG REFORM). Previously, she served as Director for Indirect Taxation and Tax Administration in the Directorate-General for Taxation and Customs Union (DG TAXUD). Earlier in her career, she was Head of Unit for Financial Markets Infrastructure in the Directorate-General for Financial Stability, Financial Services and Capital Markets Union (DG FISMA). She joined the European Commission in 1997.
