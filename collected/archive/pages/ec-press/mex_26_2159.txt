Commissioner Hoekstra to convene Implementation Dialogue on the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism
Tomorrow, Commissioner Hoekstra will host an Implementation Dialogue on the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), a key EU climate policy tool to reduce the risk of carbon leakage, ensure a level playing field for EU stakeholders and encourage global decarbonisation.
The dialogue will bring together international stakeholders, including representatives of the iron and steel, aluminium, cement and fertilisers sectors, large downstream consumers and energy utilities, third-party auditing and verification bodies, and NGOs. Discussions will focus on the practical aspects of implementing CBAM and on exchanging first experiences from the definitive period, which began on 1 January 2026.
Commissioner Hoekstra highlights CBAM's importance and the timeliness of the exchange: “CBAM is a cornerstone of the EU's climate ambition, ensuring a level playing field for European industry and sending a strong global signal that the transition to a low-carbon economy must be shared by all. With the definitive period now underway and important obligations ahead in 2027, this is a crucial moment to engage with stakeholders on how the system is working in practice. CBAM's success depends on it being robust, predictable and workable for businesses, third-country partners and public authorities.”
Feedback from the dialogue will feed into the operational implementation of CBAM by taking stock of progress, best practices and challenges. A comprehensive review of CBAM will be conducted in 2027, drawing on the experience gained, lessons learned and feedback received during the first two years of its definitive application.
More information and a summary of the conclusions will be available on the dedicated webpage.
