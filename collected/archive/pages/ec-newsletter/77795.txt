Dear Participant,
Thank you for attending the webinar on Verification and Accreditation. We hope you found the session informative and useful.
Please don’t hesitate to share your thoughts with us! You can fill in the feedback survey by 30 July 2026 at the latest: LINK TO SURVEY
Also, if you have not already done so, we would also like to invite you to complete the Commission survey for verifiers by 14 August 2026: Commission survey for verifiers. This survey collects information on accreditation plans, expected verification capacity, and geographical coverage to support the implementation of the CBAM accreditation and verification framework. Participants may also opt in to receive future communications from the Commission.
Responses will be used for internal Commission purposes only. Any information shared externally will be aggregated and anonymised. Participation does not constitute, nor influence, any CBAM accreditation application.
In addition to the surveys, please find below some resources for you:
A one-pager with the key takeaways of the webinar and the PowerPoint slides will be available on the event page. Feel free to share it with any relevant colleagues who were unable to attend the event.
The recording of the webinar will be provided next week via the CBAM website.
In the meantime, please remember to subscribe to our CBAM newsletter to receive the latest updates on upcoming webinars and the latest CBAM news. You can also check the CBAM website for more information.
