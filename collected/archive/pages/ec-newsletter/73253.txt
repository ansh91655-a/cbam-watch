How to ask questions during the webinar
If you’d like to interact with us during the webinar, please feel free to ask your questions via Slido, which can be accessed directly under the streaming video player. We will do our best to respond to your questions during the dedicated Q&A sessions.
Agenda
Several experts from DG TAXUD will present an overview of the CBAM, covering its overall framework and key implementation steps. The event will explain how CBAM emissions are calculated, as well as the processes for verification and accreditation, with guidance on how to use actual verified emissions.
