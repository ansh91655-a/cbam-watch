Dear Madam/Sir,
Thank you for subscribing to the CBAM Newsletter. We would now like to consider you a dedicated CBAM Insider.
What does this mean?
A CBAM Insider will get the latest CBAM developments direct to your mailbox every few months. You will also be invited to all upcoming events and get exclusive access to the latest materials such as explainer factsheets, videos and more. We hope that you will find these materials both informative and engaging and will share them with your wider network. And, since there’s no time like the present to take up your new role, let’s get started.

Any questions?
The CBAM Helpdesk is the central hub for Insiders to ask questions regarding operational guidance, event updates, and the latest communication materials. Our primary mission is to steer you toward the right resources and ensure you’re always in the loop.
