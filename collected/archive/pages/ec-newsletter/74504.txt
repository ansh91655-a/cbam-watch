Invitation to European Commission webinar on CBAM – 7 May 2026, 13:30 – 15:00 CEST
Read the online version
23/04/2026
CARBON BORDER ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM (CBAM)
Invitation to European Commission webinar
7 May 2026, 13:30 – 15:00 CEST
Follow us
Dear Ms/Mr,
On behalf of the European Commission, we are pleased to invite you to the upcoming webinar “How to make decarbonisation efforts pay off”, organised by the European Commission’s Directorate-General for Taxation and Customs Union (DG TAXUD) on 7 May 2026, from 13:30 to 15:00 CEST.
This webinar will provide information to help exporters in a third-country master the CBAM methodology and turn CBAM compliance into a strategic advantage. Discover how to navigate the requirements effectively and ensure your low-carbon investments translate into a tangible competitive edge in the EU market.
The session will be held in English. Please submit your questions via the registration form below by 3 May 2026. We will use your input to shape the presentation and Q&A. Due to time constraints, we cannot guarantee an answer to every specific query, but we will cover the topics most relevant to all participants.
Please click here to register.
If you have a colleague who you would like to invite, please feel free to forward this invitation to them.
We invite you to register by 5 May 2026 at the latest. All registered participants will receive the link to connect to the webinar the day before.
Please do not hesitate to reach out should you have any questions.
Looking forward to welcoming you to our next CBAM info session!
Kind regards,
CBAM Campaign Secretariat
