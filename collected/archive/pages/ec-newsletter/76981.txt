Invitation – “Become a CBAM verifier” Webinar (16 July)
Read the online version
29/06/2026
CARBON BORDER ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM (CBAM)
Invitation – “Become a CBAM verifier”
Follow us
On behalf of the European Commission, we are pleased to invite you to a dedicated webinar on accreditation and verification under the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM).
The session is intended for applicants for CBAM accreditation, including EU ETS verifiers and new verifiers, and may therefore be of interest to you.
Date and time: 16 July 2026, 09:30 – 11:30 CEST
Where: Online (the link will be sent one day before the webinar)
Registration and questions:
Please register and submit your questions via the form by 8 July 2026. Input will be used to shape the presentations and Q&A.
Agenda overview
CBAM Accreditation – general information
CBAM Verification – implementation, including technical aspects
CBAM Methodology – practical elements for verifiers
Further information, including the connection details, will follow shortly.
We look forward to welcoming you on 16 July.
Kind regards,
DG TAXUD CBAM Team
