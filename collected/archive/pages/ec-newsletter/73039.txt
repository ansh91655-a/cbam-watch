Welcome to the first edition of the European Commission’s CBAM newsletter. We’re happy to launch this to keep you informed about the latest developments on the implementation of the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM).
To mark the occasion of our first edition, we’re also pleased to invite you to our first information session which will take place online on 19 March.
Where: Online
When: 19 March, 10:00 – 11:30 CET
Agenda: Several experts from DG TAXUD will present an overview of the CBAM, covering its overall framework and key implementation steps. The event will explain how CBAM emissions are calculated, as well as the processes for verification and accreditation, with guidance on how to use actual verified emissions.
Language: The session will be held in English.
Registration: webinar registration form
Please register by 17 March 2026 at the latest. All registered participants will receive the link to connect to the webinar the day before.
