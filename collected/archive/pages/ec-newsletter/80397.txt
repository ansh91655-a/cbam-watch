15/10/2026
CARBON BORDER ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM (CBAM)
Follow us
Dear CBAM Insiders,
We would like to share a selection of new practical resources designed to help you understand CBAM requirements.
You can share these resources with colleagues, members, clients and partners who may benefit from these practical explanations. When sharing on social media, include #CBAM.
From policy to practice: new CBAM webinar available
Whether you are an importer, indirect customs representative, industry representative or trade association, this webinar provides practical insights to help you better understand CBAM obligations and prepare to meet the first CBAM compliance deadline of 30 September 2027.
The session provides guidance on emissions calculations, annual CBAM declarations and CBAM certificates, while also offering an overview of the broader policy framework.
The webinar also explores upcoming developments, including the extension of CBAM to downstream products, and highlights key milestones that organisations should be aware of.
Watch the webinar on the Customs, Tax & CBAM EU Learning Portal.
New CBAM explainer video series
Looking for a quick and accessible way to understand CBAM requirements?
Our new four-part series breaks down key concepts and processes into short, accessible videos designed specifically for businesses, industry organisations and professionals involved in CBAM implementation.
Whether you are preparing for upcoming compliance obligations, supporting members with CBAM-related questions, or simply looking to better understand the mechanism, the series provides practical guidance on key topics, including annual CBAM declarations, emissions reporting, the use of actual versus default values, and CBAM certificates.
Watch the series on our website. The four videos can also be accessed individually below.
