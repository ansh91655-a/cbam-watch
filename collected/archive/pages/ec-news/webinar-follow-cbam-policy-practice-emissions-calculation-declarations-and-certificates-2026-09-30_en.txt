Webinar follow up: “CBAM: from policy to practice - emissions calculation, declarations, and certificates”
The European Commission held the fourth in a series of webinars on the implementation of the EU’s Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM).
News article
30 September 2026
Directorate-General for Taxation and Customs Union
2 min read
The webinar aimed to help businesses understand the broader policy context of CBAM as well as the practical steps to prepare for the first compliance deadline of 30 September 2027. By this date, the first CBAM declaration must be submitted and the corresponding CBAM certificates surrendered.
The webinar started with a panel discussion emphasising that CBAM is designed to deter carbon leakage and ensure fair competition in Europe.
Following an overview of CBAM and its simplifications, the webinar covered the framework's upcoming extension to downstream products and its key implementation tools and milestones.
