New training paths available for Taxation and Customs officers from candidate and potential candidate countries
Discover the new available training paths for taxation and for customs officers on the EU Learning Potal.
News announcement
27 August 2026
Directorate-General for Taxation and Customs Union
1 min read
Two curricula modules for taxation and for customs officers from candidate and potential candidate countries have been released on the DG TAXUD Customs, Tax & CBAM EU Learning Portal. These modules are gateways that guide and provide direct access to targeted training resources designed for taxation officers or customs officers in candidate and potential candidate countries.
The modules feature links to selected courses of the training catalogue of the portal, displayed in a pre-determined order, to provide an extensive knowledge on the EU customs and taxation acquis and its implementation. Taxation and customs officers can also select a specific course according to their competence and/or need.
