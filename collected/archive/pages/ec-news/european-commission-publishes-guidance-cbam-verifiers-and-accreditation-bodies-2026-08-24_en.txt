The European Commission publishes guidance for CBAM verifiers and accreditation bodies
On 24 August 2026, the European Commission published new Guidance on CBAM verification and accreditation for verifiers and National Accreditation Bodies.
News article
24 August 2026
Directorate-General for Taxation and Customs Union
2 min read
The guidance support stakeholders in understanding the requirements applicable to verification and accreditation under the CBAM definitive period.
What the guidance covers
The guidance is intended to explain the requirements of the CBAM Regulation in clear language, with a particular focus on verifiers intending to verify emissions reports of operators of installations producing CBAM goods outside the EU that are imported from 1 January 2026.
The guidance will also describe the requirements applicable to National Accreditation Bodies (NABs) performing accreditation and surveillance of these verifiers.
Access to the CBAM Registry
The Commission has also published a procedure setting out how CBAM verifiers can obtain access to the CBAM Registry as from 1 September 2026 (see link below) and describes relevant roles and responsibilities of verifiers, accreditation bodies, EC and NCAs.
For further information, please visit the CBAM Registry webpage and consult the access procedure.
Accreditation by the relevant National Accreditation Body (NAB) is the first step in the process, followed by a subsequent application for an access to the CBAM Registry. The relevant National Competent Authority (NCA) would grant access to verifiers after checking that they are duly accredited.
Why it matters
The guidance will help verifiers and NABs understand the requirements applicable to verification and accreditation under CBAM, while the Registry access manual provides a clear framework for Accredited Entities seeking access to the system.
CBAM verifiers must register in the CBAM Registry within two months of receiving their CBAM accreditation, but not before 1 September 2026.
Starting from January 2027, they will issue verification reports in the CBAM Registry, thus allowing importers to use actual verified emissions to complete their CBAM declaration.
