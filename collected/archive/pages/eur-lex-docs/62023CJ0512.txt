Recital 10 of that regulation states that it is part of the ‘Fit for 55’ legislative package, which is a comprehensive set of mutually reinforcing measures and initiatives aimed at achieving climate neutrality in the Union by 2050, and is an essential element of the Union’s toolbox for meeting the objective of a climate-neutral Union at the latest by 2050, in line with the Paris Agreement.
