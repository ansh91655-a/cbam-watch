CBAM Registry
Information on the CBAM Registry and reporting requirements for importers and installation operators
PlayPause
Page contents
Page contents
The CBAM Registry is an electronic platform for importers under the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) to perform their obligations and report on them. The registry includes functionalities related to:
CBAM authorisations
reporting obligations
submissions of emissions data
access to CBAM modules and functionalities
This page provides information on the registry as well as access management, reporting obligations and operational resources for all stakeholders involved in the application of CBAM.
Accessing the registry
Access to the CBAM Registry may be required for:
importers of CBAM goods and indirect customs representatives
authorised CBAM declarants
non-EU installation operators
National Competent Authorities
All users need to set up two-factor authentication (2FA) on their EU Login account to access the registry. For assistance with the setup, watch the explainer on EU Login 2FA or consult the EU Login FAQ. An EU Login User Guide is also available.
Access EU Login
Authorisation Management Module
Importers of CBAM goods, or their indirect customs representatives, are urged to apply for the status of authorised CBAM declarants through the Authorisation Management Module (AMM). The AMM allows importers and indirect customs representatives to submit applications for the status of authorised CBAM declarant in their EU country of establishment.

Data reconciliation for monitoring and control
The data reconciliation for monitoring and control (DRMC) component monitors the CBAM declarant’s account and gives declarants a view of all data on imported goods, emissions, certificates and penalties. It also enables declarants to monitor and anticipate requirements for the 50% rule.

For non-EU installation operators
This module is intended exclusively for installation operators outside the EU. Operators of companies established within the EU should use the Authorisation Management Module (AMM).
The module for non-EU installation operators (Operators of Third-Country Installations – O3CI) allows installation operators outside the EU to upload and share their installations and emissions data with reporting declarants in a streamlined manner, instead of submitting it to each declarant separately.
Read more
O3CI allows operators to ensure confidential treatment of business-sensitive data. Reporting declarants are then able to automatically populate their CBAM reports with this emissions data to comply with their reporting obligations.
CBAM declarants can retrieve the information provided by non-EU installation operators in the CBAM Registry. Via the ‘Emissions’ tab, declarants can ‘Search in Third Country Installations registry’ and access the respective installation and emissions data. For this to work, CBAM declarants need to share their EORI number with the supplier outside the CBAM Registry.
Access CBAM Registry - O3CI portal
To become a Registered Operator, non-EU installation operators need to follow the steps outlined below and consult the dedicated e-learning modules and guidance documents.
Step 1: Create an EU Login account
Go to EU Login.
Register using your email address (a company email is strongly recommended), create a password, and confirm your registration via the email link you receive.
Set up two-factor authentication (EU Login App, QR code, or FIDO2 key).
Each user must register individually.
Step 2: Submit access request
Go to the CBAM Registery - O3CI Portal.
Log in using your EU Login credentials.
Complete the onboarding form with the following information:
Company details and registration number/identifiers
Company email and website
User role (Admin Operator or Simple Operator)
Upload a single PDF containing (documents preferably in English, including a courtesy translation, along with the original documents):
Company registration certificate (including registration number/identifier matching the one provided in the form)
Proof of representation (e.g., power of attorney)
ID of the authorised representative
Step 3: Submit access request in the CBAM portal
Go to O3CI.
Complete all the information as requested in the following Technical user manual – CBAM Registry access for non-EU installation operators
Step-by-step guidance and training materials on the use of the O3CI functionalities in the CBAM Registry, including registration procedures, access requests and user guidance for non-EU installation operators, are available in document and video form.

O3CI operators' videos
Video tutorial
Description
Manage registration requests, requests for change, parent company details and export list of installations
This training video series consists of 10 sessions covering the comprehensive workflow for operator request management and system interaction with the O3CI (Operators of Third Country Installations) platform.
The series will help operators (with standard and administrative user roles) to navigate in O3CI platform, create, save, submit, correct, and resubmit requests following Commission’s feedback. Additional functionality covers parent company management and the ability to search and export installation data.
The sessions presented follow a logical progression from initial registration to advanced features, allowing users to follow either a sequential path or select specific topics, as needed.
Manage deregistration requests
This training video series consists of 2 sessions covering the process of Deregistration request by Operator and Deregistration request by Commission (Right to be heard) within O3CI portal.
The series will help operators (with standard and administrative user roles) to initiate, edit, track and view a deregistration request. Users will learn how to request a deregistration, check the submission status, and the details with the justification for the deregistration confirming the transaction for that specific operator.
The sessions presented follow a logical progression from initiating, viewing, editing and tracking a deregistration request, up until the completion of the deregistration process.
Search, Navigate and view details of operator, installations and supporting documents
This 11-minute training video demonstrates how to effectively search, navigate, and access detailed information about operators, installations, and supporting documentation within O3CI portal.
The video will help operators to learn how to utilize the platform's pagination, sorting and filtering functionality to locate specific installation using various criteria such as installation id, installation name, country and city of establishment.
This essential navigation training ensures authorized operators can efficiently locate required information in other functionalities of the O3CI portal such as emission management.
Accredited verifiers
The O3CI portal also allows accredited verifiers to access the CBAM Registry as verifiers. Through the portal, verifiers can submit to the NCA of the country where they have been accredited their application for registration, but also connect with non-EU installation operators, review their emissions data and supporting documentation, and issue the corresponding verification report.
Read more
Although accredited verifiers and non-EU installation operators access the CBAM Registry through the same O3CI portal, they perform different functions. Operators provide their installation and emissions data, while verifiers independently review the information and supporting evidence made available by the operators.
Following the assessment, the verifier issues a verification report for the relevant installation. This enables CBAM declarants to use actual verified emissions data when fulfilling their CBAM reporting obligations.
Access CBAM Registry - O3CI portal
To become a Registered CBAM Verifier, accredited verifiers need to follow the steps outlined below and consult the dedicated e-learning modules and guidance documents.
Step 1: Create an EU Login account
Go to EU Login.
Register using your email address (a company email is strongly recommended), create a password, and confirm your registration via the email link you receive.
Set up two-factor authentication (EU Login App, QR code, or FIDO2 key).
Each user must register individually.
Step 2: Submit access request
Go to the CBAM Registry – O3CI portal
Log in using your EU Login credentials.
Complete the onboarding form with the following information:
Accreditation country and accreditation number
Company details and registration number/identifiers
Company email and website
User role (Accredited Entity - Admin User and/or Accredited Entity - Simple User)
Upload a single PDF containing (documents preferably in English, including a courtesy translation, along with the original documents):
Accreditation Certificate
Company registration documentation (including registration number/identifier matching the one provided in the form)
Proof of representation (e.g., power of attorney)
ID of the authorised representative
Step 3: Submit to the EU NCA the application to become a registered accredited verifier
Go to the CBAM Registry - O3CI portal.
Log in using your EU Login credentials.
While in the O3CI portal, complete the application to become a registered accredited verifier, as detailed in the following manuals.
Submit your application to the EU NCA of the country where you have been accredited.
Step-by-step guidance and training materials on the O3CI functionalities available to accredited verifiers, including registration procedures, access requests and user guidance for verifiers, are available in document and video form.

Additional functionality covers Operator's ability to view and accept a collaboration request from an Accredited Entity.
