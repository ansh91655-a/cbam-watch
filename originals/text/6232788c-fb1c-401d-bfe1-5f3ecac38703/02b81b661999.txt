Reminder: What is CBAM?
The Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) puts a fair price on carbon emitted during the production of
carbon-intensive goods that are entering the EU, Norway and Iceland and to encourage cleaner industrial
production in non-EU countries. Find out more.
