Users are advised that the only legally valid values are those contained in Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1740 correcting Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621, as regards Annexes I and IV thereto.
This file does not contain the default values for indirect emissions and for electricity as a CBAM good contained in Annexes II and III to Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621.
