Reminder: What is CBAM?
The Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) is the EU’s tool to put a fair price on carbon emitted during the
production of carbon-intensive goods that are entering the EU, and to encourage cleaner industrial production in
non-EU countries. Find out more.
