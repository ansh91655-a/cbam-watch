HOW CBAM CERTIFICATES WORK
#CBAM
Starting in 2027, CBAM importers must submit
their annual CBAM declaration by 30 September
for imports of CBAM goods during the preceding
year.
The CBAM declaration reports the quantity of goods
imported, their embedded emissions, and the
corresponding number of certificates.

Following declaration submission, the final step in
the CBAM compliance process is to surrender CBAM
certificates by the same deadline.
Managing your CBAM certificates is a cycle that starts
well before this annual obligation. The flowchart below
summarises the key steps of the certificate process
carried out through the CBAM Registry.

STEP 1. Quarterly purchases*
Buy and hold CBAM certificates to comply with your quarterly obligation: At the end of each quarter,
you must hold certificates corresponding to at least 50% of embedded emissions in all CBAM goods you have
imported since the start of the year.

STEP 2. Annual surrender
By 30 September of the following year, submit your annual CBAM declaration and surrender the required
CBAM certificates.

STEP 3. Excess certificates (optional)
If you have excess CBAM certificates on your CBAM account after the annual surrender...

3a. Repurchase**

3b. Cancellation

Until 31 October, you can request a buy-back
up to the number of CBAM certificates you had to
buy to meet your quarterly obligation (see Step 1).

On 1 November, two years after their purchase, the
Commission cancels excess CBAM certificates
that have not been repurchased and have expired.
The buy-back option is no longer possible.
