Both boxes must be checked for the request, except in the case
where the Company does not wish to delegate access to an
employee, in which case you should only select Admin User.

Accreditation Country Code: This field is mandatory. Select the
country code of the NAB that provided the Accreditation.

Accreditation Number: Enter the accreditation number as provided
by NAB.
Company Address: the address of the company must be completed
as it appears on the supporting document and using Latin
characters.

Company Email: It is recommended to use a corporate email
address of the company making the request.
Company Identifier: It is very important that the organization's
identification number is entered exactly as it appears on the
supporting document (company registration documentation).
Otherwise, the application will be rejected.
Company ISO-2 Country Code: this field refers to the company’s
country of registration nomenclature according to the ISO standard.
Company Website: this field is not mandatory, but it is a
recommendation to facilitate the application.

Please note that EU access is a first step, whose exclusive purpose is to become a user to get access to the CBAM registry. This step is a prerequisite
to request access to the CBAM registry as an accredited verifier.

The second step for you is to request registration as a Registered Accredited Verifier in the CBAM registry. The decision to grant such registration is to
be taken by the National Competent Authority of the country of accreditation.
