#CBAM

CARBON BORDER ADJUSTMENT MECHANISM:
ACTUAL VS DEFAULT VALUES IN YOUR DECLARATION
CBAM requires EU importers of certain goods to become
authorised CBAM declarants, report the embedded
carbon emissions in these goods, and meet related
financial obligations. By 30 September 2027, importers
of CBAM goods must submit their first CBAM
declaration - covering embedded emissions from
2026 imports - and surrender the corresponding
CBAM certificates.

Importers can report emissions using default values
provided by the Commission or actual values. If an
importer chooses to report actual values, the producer
in the third country must provide verified emissions
data on the total embedded emissions.
The decision tree below can help importers follow the
correct steps.
