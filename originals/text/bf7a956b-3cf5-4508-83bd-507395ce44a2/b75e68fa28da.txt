1. INTRODUCTION
1.1

DOCUMENT PURPOSE

EU and non-EU companies who are accredited entities for CBAM reasons by EU Member States National
Accreditation Bodies (NABs) and would like to become Registered CBAM Accredited Verifiers, must have
access to the CBAM Third Country Operator Portal to register and report information about their company
and their accreditation scopes.
Therefore, the purpose of this document is to provide the access request steps that should be followed by
EU or non-EU Companies to get access to the CBAM Third Country Operators portal (CBAM O3CI portal)
and also how to apply in the portal towards the NCAs to become Registered CBAM Accredited Verifier.
After the access request is approved, the Accredited CBAM Verifier will be able to access the CBAM Third
Country Operator Portal, where he should perform additional steps to apply and become a registered CBAM
Accredited Verifier and review the monitoring approach, emissions calculations and supporting evidence.
These steps are described on the CBAM Registry – User manual for the O3CI Portal, available on the
CBAM Registry website.
The specific portal to be used by the Accredited Entities for the management of their registration requests
is the CBAM Registry – O3CI Portal – available at the website address:
https://cbam.ec.europa.eu/o3cinstallation.

1.2

DISCLAIMER

Please, note that EU access is a first step, to request the technical user access. This step is a prerequisite to
request access to the CBAM registry.
The second step is for your business request for registration as a Registered Verifier in the CBAM registry.
This step will be managed by the National Competent Authority of the country of accreditation.

2.

BUSINESS GUIDANCE ON THE CBAM PROCESSES

2.1

INTRODUCTION

To ensure compliance with the CBAM Regulation, the Accredited Entities and Third Country
Operators Portal (CBAM O3CI) section of the CBAM Registry has been established.
This portal allows the verifiers:
-

To apply to the EU National Competent Authorities (NCAs) to become a Registered
CBAM Accredited Verifier;
To start a collaboration request between the Registered Accredited Verifier and the
Registered Operator of Third Country Installations;
To verify operator’s emissions report calculation and to issue the verification report,
corresponding to yearly emissions for that installation.

Implementation in the CBAM Registry of the CBAM verification in third country installation was
done based on the following legislative acts:
-

Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2546 - on the application of verification
principles;
Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2551 - on accreditation and verification;
Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2547 - on methods for the calculation
of emissions embedded in goods.

Please note that Article 14(4) of the CBAM Regulation addresses confidentiality, specifying that
the CBAM registry data under Article 10(2) shall remain confidential. However, names, addresses,
and contact information of operators and the locations of installations in third countries may be
made public. Operators have however the option to withhold their names, addresses, and contact
information from public access.
2.2

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS TO BE SUBMITTED BY THE ACCREDITED VERIFIERS FOR THE
ACCESS REQUEST

When submitting the request for access as an Accredited Entity (Accredited Verifier) to the CBAM
- EU Access system to request access, please prepare and submit the following business types of
documents:
•

Accreditation Certificate: Demonstrates that the accredited entity applied to be an
accredited verifier and that the NAB provided it with this document and includes essential
details (name, address, contact information, and tax ID or equivalent).

•

Company registration documentation: Demonstrates that the company that will be
accredited as verifier provided the legally relevant documentation that identifies the
company as a registered legal entity in the country of origin.

•

Identity document of the person acting on behalf of the accredited entity: Identity
document of the representative, like national identity card or passport.

2.3

GUIDANCE FOR KEY ACCREDITED ENTITY IDENTIFIERS
•

Accredited Entity Identifier – Accredited Entity ID/Corporate Registration Number
(CRN): Accredited Entity Identifier represents the corporate registration number. This is a
unique identifier assigned to a business entity upon its official registration with a national
or regional business registry. This number serves as proof of a company's legal existence
and is used for identification and record-keeping purposes by government agencies,
financial institutions, and regulatory authorities. The format of this identifier varies by
country, often consisting of a series of numbers, letters, or a combination that uniquely
identifies the business. The company identifier should start with the 2-letter country code
for the country where the company is registered. The maximum length of this identifier is
25 characters.

•

Please note that the Accredited Entities located in a country of the European Union may
declare either their Tax identification number or their Corporate Registration number at the
Registry of Commerce in the Member State in this field.
Example identifiers (not to be used in your request):
o

BEABCD12345 (BE for the 2-letter country-code for Belgium, followed by some
letters and numbers)

o

IN123456ABCD (IN for the 2-letter country-code for India, followed by some
letters and numbers)

o

BR.XYZ-1234567890 (BR for the 2-letter country-code for Brazil, followed by a
separator (dot), followed by other characters.

We recommend using the following identifiers for the countries listed below:
For China: Unified Social Credit (USC) number
For India: Corporate Identification Number (CIN) or Limited Liability Partnership
Identification Number (LLPIN)
For the United Kingdom: Company Registration Number (CRN)
For the United States: Central Index Key (CIK)
For South Korea: Business registration number (TIN)
For Türkiye: Vergi Kimlik Numarası
For Ukraine: Registration number (EDRPOU)
For others: Business or trade register number, tax identification number, or Legal
Entity Identifier (LEI)

Accredited Entity can gain access to the CBAM Definitive Registry and then apply to become Regitered
Verifier to the National Competent Authority (NCA) of the country where they have been accredited by a
National Accreditation Body (NAB).
Once the Accredited Verifier is a Registered verifier, then they may start a collaboration request – through
the CBAM Registry – with the Operators of Third Country Installations. Aim of this collaboration is for
the Operators to communicate to the Accredited Verifier the emissions report and for the Verifier to produce
the Verification Report in the CBAM Registry.
Once the Verification Report is produced, then the Operator of Third Country Installation may share the
verified emissions information through the CBAM Registry with the importer in the EU who is authorised
CBAM declarant.
As a final step, the EU Authorised CBAM declarant, in the CBAM Registry – Declaration Lifecycle
Management module – will lodge their yearly CBAM Declaration using verified actual emissions shared
by the Operator from the Verification Report.

4. USER RESPONSIBILITIES AND SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
To ensure the security of the O3CI Portal, all users, including Accredited Entity (ACEO) and their delegated
employees (ACEEMPL), must adhere to specific responsibilities and security requirements. The European
Commission invests significant time and resources in implementing effective controls to mitigate risks and
vulnerabilities. However, the security of information also relies on the care exercised by users in their dayto-day roles.

4.1

USER RESPONSIBILITIES

O3CI Administrator has specific tasks and responsibilities within the O3CI Portal. These responsibilities
include managing their accounts, installations, and user delegations efficiently. The key tasks and
responsibilities of O3CI Administrator are as follows:
•

Request for Registration: Accredited Entities can submit a request for registration to the National
Competent Authority of the country where the National Accreditation Body has accredited the
respective verifier. Verifier will send details of their company, accreditation scope and
accreditation certificate, making this information available to the National Competent Authorities.

•

Delegation of Users: Accredited Entity Administrator can delegate access rights to the CBAM
Registry - O3CI Portal for their employees without requiring additional approvals.

The Accredited Entity Administrator is obliged to provide true and valid information in their access requests
and submissions.
By fulfilling these tasks and responsibilities, Accredited Entity Administrator gains access to the O3CI
Portal where they submit information to the National Competent Authorities. Only the National Competent
Authorities are solely responsible for the verification of information submitted by the Accredited Verifiers
during the Registration process.

4.2

the following security requirements:
•

Users must follow the principles of least privilege, need to know, need to use.
